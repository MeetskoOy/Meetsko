The problem

There's no calendar visibility across organisations.

Outlook only shows you when people in your own organisation are free — there's nowhere to see when the client's leadership team, your partner's specialists and your own team are all available. So the time gets hunted down by email, polls and calls between assistants.

Today: email ping-pong

  1. You propose three slots to eight people from three organisations.
  2. Replies trickle in over several days, some via assistants.
  3. None of the slots works for everyone. Another round.
  4. By the time a slot is agreed, someone has already booked it.
Typically days, not minutes

With Meetsko

  1. Invite participants to the project once — each connects their own calendar.
  2. See everyone's availability, up to date, on one timeline.
  3. Pick the best of the suggested slots.
  4. The invite goes out from your email and lands in your Outlook calendar.

Privacy

We see when you're busy. Never with what.

Company calendars are confidential: client names, deal code names, hiring. That's why Meetsko strips event content before anything is stored.

  • Content is stripped on the server. Only status and start and end times are kept. Titles and other meeting details are never stored on the server.
  • Everyone stays in control. Access is granted from each person's own account and can be revoked at any time — which ends calendar sharing and deletes your data.
  • Write access only on request. Meetsko writes to a calendar only if the participant explicitly allows sending invites in that project.
  • Encrypted credentials. Calendar connection tokens are stored encrypted on the server, not in the browser.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? We're happy to walk you through it in a demo.

Does the client's IT department need to approve it?

Usually not. Each participant connects only their own calendar from their own account, and Meetsko doesn't request organisation-wide permissions. If an organisation restricts user consent to apps, an administrator can approve Meetsko once for the whole organisation.

What can other participants see in my calendar?

Only when you're busy and when you're free. Event titles, locations, descriptions and attendees are removed before anything is stored, and are never shown to anyone. You can also limit which of your time slots are visible to a project.

What if a participant doesn't connect their calendar?

They're shown in the project as "Availability not shared", and shared free time is calculated from the participants who have connected. You can still invite them to meetings as usual.

Which calendars are supported?

Microsoft 365 and Outlook, with both work and personal Microsoft accounts. Google Calendar support is on the roadmap.

Is the invite sent by Meetsko or from my own email?

From your own email. The meeting is created in your Outlook with you as the organiser, so replies, changes and cancellations work exactly as they always do in Outlook.

Can I revoke access?

Yes, at any time from your own settings. Revoking immediately stops calendar reads and change monitoring, and deletes your existing calendar times from Meetsko.
