Privacy policy

1. Controller and roles

Service providerMeetsko Oy
ServiceMeetsko

Meetsko Oy processes personal data in two roles:

  • As a processor (Article 28 GDPR) for data processed in a customer organisation's projects. The customer organisation is the controller and decides how the service is used in its projects. Meetsko Oy processes this data only on the customer's instructions and under a data processing agreement.
  • As a controller for user accounts, service operation, security and logs, the customer relationship and invoicing.

Customer agreements include a separate data processing agreement (DPA) covering the subject matter, duration, instructions, sub-processors and assistance obligations.

2. The service

A lead organisation creates a project and invites participants from its own and other organisations. Each participant authorises Meetsko themselves with their own Microsoft account. Nobody shares another person's calendar on their behalf, and no organisation-wide calendar permission is required from IT administrators.

Meetsko shows participants' free and busy times, suggests times that suit everyone, and can send a meeting invitation from the organiser's own Outlook calendar. The content of calendar events (title, location, description, other attendees) is neither shown nor stored.

5. Sources of data

  1. The user: profile details, visibility restrictions and choices made in the service.
  2. Microsoft: name, email address and calendar availability, always on the basis of the user's own authorisation.
  3. The inviter or an organisation administrator: the invitee's name and email address.

Inviters must make sure they have grounds to process the invitee's contact details. Before authorising, the invitee sees who invited them and to which project.

6. Protecting calendar content

Meetsko processes only the time and status of calendar entries, never their content. This is enforced as a technical control, not merely a policy.

Calendar dataProcessed?
Start and end timeYes
Status (free / busy)Yes; tentative and out of office are shown as busy
Event titleNo, removed on the server before storage
Location and meeting linkNo, removed on the server before storage
Description, attachments, other attendeesNot retrieved
Events marked privateShown only as busy time, with nothing identifying

Meetsko requests read access to the user's own Microsoft calendar (Calendars.Read), because read access is needed to retrieve availability. As this permission would technically allow access to event content, the server strips everything except time and status as soon as the calendar response arrives, before anything is stored or sent to a browser.

Users can restrict, per project, when their calendar is visible (visibility windows and hidden periods). Hidden time is shown to others as busy and is not used for time suggestions.

Changes to Outlook calendars are signalled through Microsoft change notifications. A notification only says that the calendar has changed; availability is then fetched again through the same filter.

7. Creating events and Outlook sync

  • Write access only on request. Only a participant who has separately granted write access to their calendar in the project (Calendars.ReadWrite) can send meeting invitations. Read authorisation does not grant write access.
  • Own calendar only. The invitation is created in the organiser's own calendar with their own authorisation, and Outlook sends the invitations as usual. Meetsko never writes to other participants' calendars or uses another user's authorisation.
  • Only the organiser can make changes. An event created in Meetsko can be cancelled from the app only by its organiser.
  • Sync. For events created in Meetsko, only the time and cancellation status are read from the organiser's calendar to keep the project's event list up to date. The title, message and attendee list are never read from Outlook.
  • Visibility. Events are visible to project participants unless the creator chooses not to show the event to others. Drafts are visible only to their author. Invitees' responses (accepted / declined) are not shown.

Why does Microsoft call write access “full access”?

When you grant write access, Microsoft asks for consent to the Calendars.ReadWrite permission, described as “Have full access to your calendars” (“Have full access to user calendars” in the organisation's Entra ID). The name and description are Microsoft's standard wording, which apps cannot change. Microsoft offers no narrower permission that lets an app create a meeting invitation in a user's calendar, so every app that creates invitations on a user's behalf uses the same permission.

Because the permission would technically allow access to every event in your calendar, Meetsko's use of it is restricted in the application code:

  • New events only at your request. An event is created only when you send an invitation from Meetsko yourself, and only in your own calendar.
  • Changes only to Meetsko's own events. Cancellations and other changes apply only to events that Meetsko has created itself.
  • Other events are left alone. Meetsko never edits, deletes or cancels any other event in your calendar, and their content is not read (see section 6). For Meetsko's own events, only the time and cancellation status are read.
  • Your calendar, your request. Your permission is used only for your own requests and only for your own calendar. Another user's permission is never used to write.
  • Optional and per project. Write access is granted separately in the project settings and is not needed for viewing schedules. Without it, Meetsko copies the attendees so you can create the invitation yourself in Outlook.
  • Revocable. When you remove the authorisation in Meetsko's settings, Meetsko deletes its stored calendar connection (read and write) and can no longer reach your calendar. You can also remove the consent in your Microsoft account's app settings.

Your organisation's IT can restrict user consent with Entra ID consent policies if it wishes. Meetsko's scheduling also works with read access alone.

The main rule: calendar data is visible only to participants in the same project. People outside a project cannot see its participants' data, except for the administrators listed below.

9. Emails and reminders

  • Project invitations say who sent the invitation, to which project, and what the participant is asked to do.
  • Authorisation reminders. Project administrators can remind a participant who has not yet authorised their calendar. Meetsko also sends a limited number of automatic reminders, which organisation administrators can switch off.
  • Organisation notifications, such as being made an administrator or given the right to create projects, and, where needed, a notice to the customer's IT contact about the service being taken into use.

Emails are not used for marketing.

12. Security

Data protection

  • Traffic is encrypted with TLS 1.2 or later, and encrypted connections are enforced (HSTS).
  • The database is encrypted at rest (AES-256).
  • Calendar credentials are also encrypted at application level (AES-GCM) with a key kept separately from the database.
  • Calendar content is stripped on the server before storage (section 6).

Access control

  • Sign-in through Microsoft Entra ID: Meetsko never sees passwords, and your organisation's multi-factor authentication (MFA) and conditional access policies apply.
  • Only per-user delegated permissions to the user's own calendar; no organisation-wide application permissions are requested.
  • Role-based access control, checked on the server for every request. Customers' and projects' data is kept separate.
  • Project actions and support access are recorded in the activity log. Access to production data is limited to named people.

Infrastructure

  • Established cloud providers' infrastructure, with the database in an EU data centre (Ireland).
  • DDoS protection from the providers.

13. Cookies and browser storage

Meetsko does not use tracking, analytics or advertising cookies, and there is no third-party tracking on its pages. Fonts and other page resources are served from Meetsko's own service.

Only data that the service needs in order to work is stored in the browser: the app's sign-in session, the chosen language and view settings. It is not used for tracking, so no separate consent is requested. You can delete it in your browser settings; deleting the sign-in session signs you out.

14. Your rights

You have the right to access your data (Art. 15), to have inaccurate data rectified (Art. 16) and data erased (Art. 17), to restrict processing (Art. 18), to receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21). You can withdraw your calendar authorisation at any time.

Where Meetsko Oy acts as a processor, requests should primarily be addressed to the customer organisation, which is the controller. Meetsko Oy forwards any requests it receives to the customer without delay and helps with the response.

Requests: support@meetsko.com. We respond within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman in Finland (tietosuoja.fi) or with the supervisory authority in your own country.

15. Personal data breaches

Meetsko Oy notifies the customer of any personal data breach it becomes aware of without undue delay, as set out in the data processing agreement. As a controller, Meetsko Oy notifies the supervisory authority within 72 hours where required (Art. 33) and the data subjects if the breach is likely to result in a high risk (Art. 34).

16. Changes to this policy

This policy is updated as the service evolves and legislation changes. Customers are notified of material changes in advance.
